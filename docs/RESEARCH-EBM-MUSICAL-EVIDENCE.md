# EBM bassline research: evidence register (2026-10-13)

**Status:** research, not an artist transcription and not proof of musical output quality. Scope: minimal/early EBM through more syncopated and melodic sequenced bass. The existing v0.1.0 generator was implemented prematurely; no genre-derived statistical parameters have yet been measured.

## Evidence classes
- **DOCUMENTED (direct producer account):** Robert Görl on DAF, interview (2018). Görl explicitly describes an 8- or 32-step sequence, straight danceable beats, often a single sequence, and taking a day to make one sequence work. https://monkeypress.de/2018/04/interaktiv/interviews/interview-robert-goerl/
- **DOCUMENTED (direct producer account):** Görl describes DAF's core as one strong sequence, one beat and vocals; pitch variation is possible, not mandatory. https://www.fazemag.de/non-je-parle-francais-ein-interview-mit-robert-goerl-von-daf/
- **DOCUMENTED (direct performer interview, historical copy):** Douglas McCarthy describes making Nitzer Ebb's Lightning Man by beginning with a straight sequence, shuffling timing and removing notes. The archived page is a mirror; original publication provenance should still be confirmed. https://newtotse.com/oldtotse/en/ego/can_you_dance_to_it/ne.html
- **DOCUMENTED (direct artist interview):** Bon Harris used one SH-101 sequence memory on the first Nitzer Ebb album and developed later live material in a non-linear way, sometimes retaining original material while re-performing and rearranging. https://www.bitwig.com/artists/bon-harris-nitzer-ebb-2/
- **DOCUMENTED (direct producer interview):** Front 242's Daniel Bressanutti emphasizes sequence as an early compositional skeleton and subsequent sonic development. https://www.idieyoudie.com/2012/04/26/interview-daniel-b-of-nothing-but-noisefront-242/
- **DOCUMENTED (direct producer interview):** Daniel B explicitly considers the playback system while designing basslines; sound design can strongly transform the outcome. https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/front-242-boot-camp-click/ and https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/front-line/93
- **SECONDARY; NOT AUTHORITATIVE TRANSCRIPTION:** Fan bass-tab of Front 242's No Shuffle (dated 1998): recurring six low notes and two higher answers in each eight-slot bar, with a change in the low note and retention of the upper note positions across groups of bars. Useful as a *hypothesis* about pedal and invariant response; the original performance and pitch/timing accuracy have not been independently verified. Several websites reproduce the SAME tab and are not independent confirmations. https://musicnoteslib.com/tabs/Front_242-No_Shuffle-119904.html
- **DOCUMENTED (tutorial, not original band transcription):** EBM construction walkthrough gives a particular piano-roll seed plus velocity-sensitive sound and an arpeggiator that transforms playback. The displayed piano-roll image must not be mistaken for the resulting sounding notes. https://www.studiobrootle.com/ebm-bassline-tutorial-ableton/
- **MEASURED IN PUBLISHED LISTENER EXPERIMENTS, NOT EBM-SPECIFIC:** Moderate syncopation can increase groove versus none or very high amounts; effect depends on the actual pattern. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4165312/
- **MEASURED IN PUBLISHED LISTENER EXPERIMENTS, NOT EBM-SPECIFIC:** Original syncopation improved groove whereas automatic random re-syncopation did not; the layout and cross-instrument interactions matter, not just a scalar degree. https://online.ucpress.edu/mp/article/39/5/503/182325/Syncopation-and-Groove-in-Polyphonic-MusicPatterns

## Defensible deductions (hypotheses, not literal historic facts)
1. A short, purposefully composed rhythmic-melodic cell must be the unit of generation; the 4/8-bar export is an *arrangement* of the cell, not 4/8 independent drafts.
2. Algorithmic transformations must act on a musical role (pickup, accent, response, tension, resolution, rest). Mutation independent of role is not a reliable design.
3. Drum coupling is part of the intended composition. Co-onset with a kick is **sometimes** desirable (impact) and sometimes leaving room is desirable (answer/anticipation). A universal 'bass must avoid kicks' rule would be just as misguided.
4. Velocity and gates are part of note identity; equal pitches with changed gate/accent can constitute a meaningful alternative phrase.
5. Sound design significantly changes perceived groove; audition both with a neutral synth and representative EBM envelope/filter, with and without beat.
6. Avoid exact copying of any third-party phrase: compose entirely new test fixtures from abstract devices/relationships.

## What is still unknown
- No audited corpus of original artist MIDI or verified independent transcription. Do not state exact intervals or average notes-per-bar of any artist as fact.
- No EBM-specific, scientifically measured optimum for note density, BPM, syncopation score, or motif length. Proposed values belong to the EMPIRICALLY TUNED class until evaluated.
- DARK ELECTRO and other proposed subprofiles still need independently demonstrated distinctions. The names alone do not establish authenticity.
- Neither a legal commercial MIDI arrangement nor an unverified fan tab is an authoritative record of the artist's sequencer.
- No blind listening results. Do not claim that the current engine has proved musical utility.
